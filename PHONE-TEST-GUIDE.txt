HZ AUTO - PHONE TEST GUIDE (NO IIS)

EASIEST REAL PHONE TEST
=======================
1. Upload the contents of this folder to an HTTPS static website.
   Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages are suitable examples.
2. Open the HTTPS address in Chrome on your phone.
3. Open it once, then reload once while you still have internet.
4. Choose Breakdown Job Card and enter test information.
5. Sign the signature boxes with your finger.
6. Tap Review Job Card.
7. Tap Download PDF first and confirm the PDF looks correct.
8. Go back to review and tap Share PDF.
9. Choose Gmail, Outlook or WhatsApp from your phone's share menu.
10. Choose the recipient inside that app and press Send.

NO HOME SERVER IS INVOLVED.
Your computer can be completely turned off while you are on the road.

IF YOU DO NOT WANT TO HOST ANYTHING YET
========================================
You can copy this folder to a phone and try opening index.html locally.
PDF download may work, but browser security rules can prevent the Share PDF
feature and PWA/offline features from working properly from a local file.
For normal roadside use, HTTPS static hosting is the recommended option.
